    How it ranks

    Each listing has an editorial rating from 1 to 5. That rating is multiplied by your interest in its category, and listings with a For you note get a bonus. Don't miss and Recommended come from that score; the top strip also favours what is soonest.

    For you notes draw on your journal (opera as drama, Wagner and Strauss, Russian repertoire, émigré memoirs, Flemish painting, a feminist reading of books) and on what you read and care about now: contemporary fiction, Galicia and its language and culture, languages and linguistics, AI, chess, and animals.

    Items marked to confirm have incomplete or conflicting details in the sources. Check the venue before buying.
